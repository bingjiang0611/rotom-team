Its two production callers are `Workspace.switchBranch/revertChange`, but both first require the retired Durable driver, so they do not establish a reachable native feature. Preserve its backend/attribution scenario, exact assertions and delayed-count ordering; do not delete the test, fake a model result, or run a legacy task. This removes the final note execution body without retaining it solely to manufacture fixture data. All other old test files remain unchanged.

B7's originally named `stopUser` method does not exist. The inspected legacy loop is `PocketApp.projectsChanged`, following `kernel.revoke`, client eviction and session-list refresh. Only the loop invoking Durable `conversation.abort` is to be removed; the native revoke and authorization refresh remain. B6 also removes `Shell.stop`'s task lookup/abort success body and its unreferenced `printed` stub, while retaining the method's refusal boundary and real read-only `describe`.

All named groups include only their newly unused imports and private helpers.
